Casefiles

Product checks written like operational proof.

Useproof casefiles are meant to be reviewed by humans and executed by machines. They describe the goal, the agent task, the browser settings, and the exact evidence required for a pass.

checkout.case.yml
case: checkout-smoke-test
objective: Prove that checkout reaches an order confirmation.

agent:
  task: Log in, add the demo product to cart, and complete checkout with the test card.
  model: auto
  max_steps: 25

browser:
  headless: true
  viewport:
    width: 1440
    height: 1100

verdict:
  pass_when:
    - page_contains: Order confirmed
    - url_matches: /orders/
    - capture: confirmation_number

receipts:
  - screenshot
  - run_tape
  - browser_trace
  - dossier

Expected dossier

The dossier should show the objective, final verdict, agent timeline, receipts, and captured confirmation number.

  • Final URL and page title
  • Screenshot at verdict time
  • Step timeline with observations
  • Machine-readable result JSON

Use cases worth productizing first

Login and auth

Check sign-in, reset links, SSO routing, dashboard arrival, and account state.

Checkout

Check product selection, cart state, payment handoff, confirmation screens, and receipts.

Admin and support

Check internal workflows that are too expensive to keep brittle in a hand-coded E2E suite.
